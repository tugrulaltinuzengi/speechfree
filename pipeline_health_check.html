Key Finding — EMG Electrode Noise

⚠️ EMG1 noise floor overlaps speech signal in kayit1
MeasureValue
Calibration EMG env. mean0.559 V
Calibration EMG env. std0.062 V
Raw ADC range during silence9 – 900 / 1023
Threshold at k=3.00.743 V
Threshold at k=1.50.651 V
HAYIR peak EMG env.0.656 V — only 5 mV margin at k=1.5
DUR peak EMG env.0.769 V — comfortably above k=3.0

Root cause: EMG electrode picks up near-full ADC range broadband noise during silence (std ≈ 260 ADC steps = 25% of 10-bit range). After 20–200 Hz bandpass, a significant noise floor (~0.56 V envelope) remains.


Consequence: Short / low-effort words (HAYIR, EVET) produce EMG only ~10–20% above the noise floor. Any single k cannot simultaneously capture low-SNR words and suppress silence FPs.


k=1.5 tradeoff: captures 66% of REPs but generates 69 FPs. k=3.0 only captures ~40% of REPs with fewer FPs.


Next step: Check kayit2–4. If EMG quality improves (electrode re-attachment, shorter session), k=3.0 may work. PZT2 shows 6–24× better SNR for EVET/HAYIR but inconsistent for MERHABA.
